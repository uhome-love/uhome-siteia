- Jetimob sync: full daily scan (deactivates removed listings) + incremental run of first/last listing pages triggered by the watchdog tick (about every 10 min, only when no full scan is running); Jetimob lists by registration date, so new listings land on the last pages.
- A DB trigger keeps existing imoveis coordinates when the sync sends null, so geocoded locations survive re-syncs.
- Campaign/"Uhome em Foco" products live in `produtos_foco` (public content in `ficha` jsonb, images as Lovable Assets URLs) and `campanhas` + `campanha_produtos`; kept separate from legacy `empreendimentos` so old pages keep working. Campaign visibility and Black week are date-driven (America/Sao_Paulo) in `src/lib/produtosFoco.ts`, so campaigns switch on/off without deploys.
- Campaign-wide offer lines (`campanhas.config.condicoes_gerais`, `config.urna`) feed both product pages and LIA tools. Why: ads, page and LIA must promise the same offer.
- Broker-personalized product pages reuse the `/c/:corretorSlug` prefix; in that mode pages show the broker card and WhatsApp instead of the lead form. `ssr-render` serves link previews for `/em-foco`, `/em-foco/:slug` and `/campanha/:slug`.
- The campaign flagged `destaque_home` (via `useCampanhaDestaque`) replaces "Uhome em Foco" in navbar, hero and `/em-foco` while vigente; after it ends `/em-foco` returns automatically. Why: one showcase per period, no deploy needed.
- Official Natal art (lights, key, seal, Homi) lives in `src/components/foco/NatalArt.tsx` as Lovable Assets; `--natal-*` tokens follow the campaign brand kit.
- Campaign price/city filtering is client-side in `campanhaFilters.ts`; keep the original catalog and broker prefixes intact so filters do not change campaign membership or attribution.
- Product photos, floor plans and maps share `MaterialGallery` with Radix focus management, keyboard navigation, swipe and zoom; this keeps material inspection consistent without altering source assets.
- LIA on the site: `lia-chat` edge function (Responses API + tools over imoveis/produtos_foco) writes leads/visits to `public_leads`/`agendamentos`, which reach the CRM only via existing DB triggers; prompt rules mirror the CRM LIA brain. Why: one LIA, one lead pipeline.
- Site leads go to the CRM "Fila do CEO" (pipeline_leads pendente_distribuicao) in `sync-to-crm` only when the person (same phone/email) has no active broker, or is discarded/archived; if an active broker exists, only `dados_site`/observacoes are appended (rule in `sync-to-crm/distribuicao.ts`). Why: owner distributes new site leads manually, and existing broker work must not be reset.
- Visitor identity (phone/email) is remembered in localStorage via `setLeadIdentity` in `src/lib/session.ts` at every contact capture; the `site-identidade` function links prior anonymous `lead_events` and sends a history summary to the CRM. Why: CRM only gets site activity from identified people.
- Lead tracking (first-touch UTM/click IDs, page, form, device, behavior summary) is built only in `src/lib/leadTracking.ts` and attached to every lead insert; sync-to-crm forwards it in `dados_site`. Why: one source so every form sends the same context to the CRM.
- New-lead email alerts are sent from sync-to-crm through the linked Gmail connection (owner's own mailbox), skipped for names containing TESTE. Why: no verified sender domain needed.
- WhatsApp clicks are captured globally in `src/services/whatsappTracker.ts` (document click capture + `window.open` wrapper), stored in `whatsapp_clicks` with keepalive fetch, and forwarded by sync-to-crm to the CRM `receive-landing-lead` as `tipo: clique_whatsapp`. Why: every button is covered without per-button code and WhatsApp never waits for the send.
- Product pages `/em-foco/:slug` use their own slim header/footer (ad landing mode) and "Agendar visita" as primary CTA via `AgendaVisitaFoco` (agendamentos + submitLead). Optional `ficha.motivos/faq/frase` and gallery `categoria` override auto-derived content. Why: paid traffic lands on a focused page without site navigation leaks.
