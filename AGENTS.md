Why: every button is covered without per-button code and WhatsApp never waits for the send.

- Campaign product photos may carry an optional `url_mobile` (≤800 px) used via `fotoResponsiva` srcset; originals stay untouched for desktop and zoom. Why: keep every mobile image under 200 KB without altering source assets.
